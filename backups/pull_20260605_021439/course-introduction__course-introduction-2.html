Phase 1 Foundations: Concepts and Intuition M1–M4  ·  15% of grade  ·  Details ▼

No code. No prior experience. No assumed knowledge. You begin with the question every student should be able to answer but rarely can: what does artificial intelligence actually mean? By the end of Phase 1, you will have a working vocabulary, a critical lens, and enough intuition to spot AI in the wild.

→  What is artificial intelligence, and what is just automation?
→  How do machines learn from data?
→  Build and test a classifier with no code using real browser-based tools
→  Bias, fairness, privacy, and the ethics of automated decision-making

By the end of Phase 1: You will be able to explain how AI works to anyone, including adults who assume they already know.

Phase 2 Code Lab: Python, Data, and Machine Learning M5–M9  ·  25% of grade  ·  Details ▼

Python is the language behind most of the world's AI infrastructure. You will learn it from scratch, not through abstract exercises, but through working with real datasets, asking real questions, and building a machine learning model that actually runs. No prior coding experience is assumed.

→  Python fundamentals with AI-assisted debugging
→  Loading, cleaning, and exploring real-world datasets
→  Statistics that underpin machine learning decisions
→  Supervised learning: training, testing, and evaluating a model
→  Build and submit your first machine learning model

By the end of Phase 2: You will have a working ML model and enough Python to continue well beyond this course.
Phase 3 Modern AI: Applications in the Real World M10–M14  ·  25% of grade  ·  Details ▼

This is where the course turns to the systems everyone is talking about, and where critical thinking matters most. You will learn how image recognition, language models, and generative AI actually work. You will also learn to evaluate their outputs rigorously and catch them when they fail.

→  Computer vision: how machines interpret images
→  Language AI: classification, tone, and ambiguity
→  Large language models: architecture, hallucinations, and limitations
→  Prompting: design, evaluation, and red-teaming
→  Agentic AI: goals, memory, tool use, and guardrails

By the end of Phase 3: You will know how to use AI tools professionally and how to think critically about everything they produce.
Phase 4 Launch Pad: Build and Showcase M15–M18  ·  35% of grade  ·  Details ▼

Every preceding phase was preparation for this one. You will design, build, and present a working AI-powered web app. There is no template to copy, no predefined outcome. Your idea, your architecture, your code. You present it to a real audience and explain every decision you made.

→  Vibe coding: AI-assisted development from concept to working prototype
→  Full-stack AI project I: architecture, user flow, and system design
→  Guided build II: integrate AI, handle errors, and test rigorously
→  Showcase: present your web app and defend your design choices

By the end of Phase 4: A portfolio-ready web app and the ability to explain every decision you made in building it.

The Structure

Every module follows the same rhythm.

Learn the pattern once. You will always know exactly where you are and what comes next, across all 18 modules.

1

Read and watch

Concise readings and 3–5 minute micro-videos. One concept per video. A live comprehension check is embedded mid-video. You cannot skip past it.
2

Complete the activity

Each lesson includes a hands-on task: meme challenges, sorting exercises, hallucination hunts, real-world audits. Engaging and always tied directly to what you just learned.
3

Self-check

A short, ungraded quiz. Not an assessment, but a confidence check. It confirms you are ready before the next lesson unlocks.
4

Contribute to the discussion

One real-world question per lesson. Post your perspective, then engage substantively with two classmates. Discussion is where understanding deepens.
5

Save your work

Every module adds one artifact to your portfolio. By the final module, you will have a complete record of how your thinking evolved, from your first lesson to a fully working web app.

Assessment

Designed to reward growth, not just performance.

The grade structure is intentionally weighted toward the later phases, where the work is hardest and the skills are most developed. Early struggle is expected. It will not define your final result.

Before You Begin

Common questions, answered directly.

Do I need prior coding experience? ▼

No. The first four modules contain no code at all. You will write your first line of Python in Module 5. By that point you will have enough context about how computers think that it will make intuitive sense. Students who have coded before will progress faster, but they will not find the course trivial.

Am I permitted to use AI tools to assist with assignments? ▼

Yes, and you are expected to. The distinction that matters is this: using an AI tool to accelerate your learning and improve your work is exactly the point of this course. Using it to bypass thinking entirely defeats the purpose of being here, and it will show in the work you submit.

How long should I expect each module to take? ▼

Plan for approximately 4 to 5 hours per module. Phase 1 modules tend to run slightly shorter. Phase 4 build sprints will take longer depending on your project scope. The course is self-paced. Consistency matters more than speed.

What software do I need to install? ▼

Nothing. Every tool used in this course runs entirely in a browser. You will use Google Colab for Python, browser-based tools for the no-code modules, and AI-assisted development platforms in Phase 4. A reliable internet connection is the only requirement.

What will this course actually prepare me for? ▼

A genuine foundation: enough to understand AI deeply, use it professionally, and build with it. Whether you pursue a career in technology or work in medicine, law, design, business, or any other field, the people who understand AI will carry a meaningful advantage. This course puts you in that position.

What We Expect

Three expectations. Nothing more.

One

Remain curious.

The right question matters more than the right answer. Every lesson rewards careful thinking over a correct guess.

Two

Use AI critically.

Use AI tools throughout the course. But verify what they produce, understand their limits, and identify when they are wrong.

Three

Save your work.

Every module includes a portfolio artifact. Do not skip it. By the final module, it becomes a complete record of your progress through the course.
