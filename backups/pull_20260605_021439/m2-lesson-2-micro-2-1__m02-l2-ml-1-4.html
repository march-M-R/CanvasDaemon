Before You Watch

Every column is a choice.

“When you select features, you are deciding what the model is allowed to learn from. Include too few and the model has nothing to work with. Include too many and you add noise, risk leakage, and slow training.”

Concept Companion

Signal. Availability. Ethics.

Three properties separate a strong feature from a weak one. The strong ones share all three. Miss any one and the column hurts more than it helps.

Connected to the target

Signal

Mechanically related to what you are predicting. Not just correlated; ideally explainable.

Known at prediction time

Availability

If the feature is only known after the outcome, it is unusable. This is the leakage trap.

Safe to use

Ethics

Not a protected attribute, not a proxy. The feature should not introduce bias or discrimination.

Compare

Strong features vs noise features.

The difference is almost always whether the feature has a mechanical link to the target, or whether it just happens to correlate by accident.
