Picking a 2-Class Problem Worth Solving

Concept Companion

Strong class pairs are distinct, capturable, and useful.

If you can capture both classes with your phone in under ten minutes, you have a workable problem. If the two classes are visually identical to a stranger, the model will struggle too.

Visually different

Distinct

A stranger looking at two random samples could tell which class is which.

You can actually collect data

Capturable

You have access to enough variety of both classes today.

Worth doing

Useful

A correct prediction would actually help someone, even in a small way.

Real-World Case

The Not Hotdog story

In 2017, the HBO show Silicon Valley joked about a 'Not Hotdog' app that classified one thing vs everything-else. A developer actually built it, shipped it on the App Store, and it got millions of downloads. Two classes, picked carefully, were enough to be useful.

Apply

Try it now.

List three 2-class problems from your daily life. Score each one on distinct, capturable, useful. Pick the highest scorer.
