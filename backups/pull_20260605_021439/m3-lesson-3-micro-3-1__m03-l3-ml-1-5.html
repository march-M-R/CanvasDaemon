False Positives and False Negatives

Wrong is not a single category. It has flavors.

Big Question

Which kind of wrong matters more in your project?
Watch → Read → Apply

Before You Watch

Same model, different costs.

Two predictions can both be wrong and yet one is a small inconvenience and the other is catastrophic. Naming which one your project hates more is the start of real model design.

Concept Companion

Four outcomes. Two of them are mistakes.

A binary classifier produces exactly four outcomes per prediction. Two are correct. Two are wrong, in opposite ways.

Said yes, was right

True Positive

The good case. Class detected correctly.

Said yes, was wrong

False Positive

A wrong alarm. Annoying. Sometimes worse.

Said no, missed it

False Negative

A missed detection. Often the costlier mistake in safety contexts.
