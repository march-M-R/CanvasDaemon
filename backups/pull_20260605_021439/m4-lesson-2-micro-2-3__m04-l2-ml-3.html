Before You Watch

Real consent has three properties.

A pop-up that says 'Accept all to continue' is not real consent. Real consent means you know what you are agreeing to, you can opt into parts of it, and you can change your mind later without being punished.

Concept Companion

Informed, specific, revocable.

Consent without all three is not consent. It is agreement under pressure.

You know what you agreed to

Informed

The terms are written so a normal reader could understand them. Plain language.

Granular, not all-or-nothing

Specific

You can opt into analytics and out of advertising separately.

You can take it back

Revocable

You can withdraw consent later and have your data deleted. No penalty.
