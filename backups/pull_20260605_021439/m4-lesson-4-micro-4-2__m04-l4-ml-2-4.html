Before You Watch

Stakes set the rule.

“Decisions that affect health, safety, freedom, or legal status should not be made by a model alone, even an accurate one. The cost of a single wrong prediction is too high to leave to a system without a human override.”

Concept Companion

Four domains. No exceptions.

These four domains have a common property: a single wrong decision is irreversible, or nearly so. Human oversight is not optional.

Diagnosis and treatment

Health

AI can support a clinician but should not replace one.

Self-driving, alerts

Safety

Humans must intervene quickly when the system is uncertain.

Hiring, loans, parole

Legal/Financial

Decisions affecting rights or livelihood need human accountability.

Compare
