MODULE 4 · OPENING DISCUSSION

Confident or correct?

Before we define fairness, test your gut.

5 Points · Graded

The Scenario

A school uses an AI tool that flags students 'likely to drop out'. The school says the tool is 88 percent accurate.

Is 88 percent enough to act on? For whom?

Think about:

• What if the 12 percent of mistakes fall mostly on one group of students?
• What does 'flagged' actually mean for the student? Is it visible? Reversible?
• Who decides whether to act on the flag, and what happens if they are also wrong?

Your Task

  1. Initial post (3 to 5 sentences): Defend a position. Should the school act on the flag? Under what conditions?
  2. Reply to TWO classmates (2 to 3 sentences each): Push them on the parts of their answer that hide assumptions.
  3. Be specific. Name who is affected, name the harm, name the mitigation.

What Good Looks Like

Example Responses

STRONG INITIAL POST (Click to expand)

88 percent is not enough on its own. The school should never act on the flag without a human counselor reviewing the student's full situation, because the 12 percent of wrong predictions almost certainly fall harder on students whose backgrounds are underrepresented in the training data. The flag should also be invisible to teachers to prevent biased grading, and the student or family should be able to ask why they were flagged and contest it.

Why this works: Names a specific harm, identifies the affected group, proposes concrete mitigations.

STRONG REPLY (Click to expand)

You said the flag should be 'invisible to teachers' but I think that hides a real problem. If teachers do not know who is at risk, they cannot offer extra help. Maybe the flag should be visible to counselors only, with a script for offering support without revealing the AI's role.

Why this works: Engages with a specific claim, surfaces a hidden tradeoff, offers a refined alternative.

WEAK POST (Click to expand)

AI is biased so the school should not use it.

Why this loses points: No specifics. No affected group. No proposed alternative.
