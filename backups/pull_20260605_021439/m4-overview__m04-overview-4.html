Module Mindset

Confident is not correct.

This week is not about being suspicious of AI. It is about being precise. Specific harms, specific groups, specific mitigations. Vague ethics is theater. This module is not theater.

Learning Outcomes

What you will be able to do

Outcome 1

Define algorithmic fairness and identify disparate impact in real AI systems.

Outcome 2

Read a privacy policy in 90 seconds and summarize its real practices in three bullets.

Outcome 3

Explain why LLMs hallucinate and recognize confident-but-wrong outputs in practice.

Outcome 4

Decide when human-in-the-loop review is mandatory versus optional in real AI deployments.

Outcome 5

Run a structured AI risk review with specific harms, affected groups, and concrete mitigations.

Module Goal

Defend or refuse an AI deployment with specific, evidence-backed reasoning.
