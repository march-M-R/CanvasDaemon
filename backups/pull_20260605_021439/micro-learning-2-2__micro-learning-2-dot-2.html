2.2 Breakthroughs That Changed AI

Major Breakthroughs

Three breakthrough waves

Wave 1

Game AI

Machines defeating expert human players in structured games showed that computers could search and plan very effectively.

Impact: AI could outperform humans in narrow, rule-based environments.

Wave 2

Deep Learning

Deep learning improved image recognition, speech recognition, translation, and recommendation systems.

Impact: AI became useful in everyday consumer technology.

Wave 3

Generative AI

Large language models and generative tools made AI conversational, creative, and accessible to everyday users.

Impact: AI became visible, interactive, and mainstream.

Open: Why breakthroughs matter

A breakthrough matters not just because the technology improved, but because it changed what people believed was possible. Each visible success increased trust, investment, attention, and adoption.
