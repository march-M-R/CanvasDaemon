Four Big Ideas

The module in one view

AI Basics

AI systems use data and patterns to perform tasks that normally require human judgment.

AI Evolution

Modern AI did not appear suddenly. It grew through decades of research, limits, and breakthroughs.

Everyday AI

Recognition, recommendation, and prediction systems shape many apps and services people use daily.

LLM Literacy

LLMs generate fluent text by prediction, so confident answers still need verification.

Check Yourself

Can you explain these?

Topic What you should be able to say
What AI is AI is not one tool. It is a broad set of systems designed to perform tasks using data, patterns, and models.
Why AI took off More data, more compute, better algorithms, and connected platforms improved at the same time.
Where AI appears AI appears in recognition, recommendations, predictions, search, media tools, assistants, and many everyday workflows.
Why verification matters AI can sound fluent and still be incomplete, biased, outdated, or wrong.

Final Habit

Use AI as a powerful assistant, not as an unquestioned authority.

The strongest AI users know how to ask good questions, interpret outputs carefully, and verify important claims.

Module 1 Takeaways

Keep these with you

AI is already part of everyday life, often behind tools people use without noticing.

Modern AI became practical because multiple technical and social conditions improved together.

Responsible AI use means checking important claims, watching for bias, and knowing when human judgment matters.
