Phase 1 Foundations: Concepts and Intuition M1–M4  ·  Details ▼

You begin with the question everyone should be able to answer clearly: what does artificial intelligence actually mean? By the end of Phase 1, you will have a working vocabulary, a critical lens, and enough intuition to recognise AI in everyday life.

→ What is artificial intelligence, and what is just automation?

→ How do machines learn from data?

→ How can you build and test a classifier with no code?

→ What do bias, fairness, privacy, and ethics look like in AI?

By the end of Phase 1: You will be able to explain the basics of AI clearly, without relying on buzzwords.

Phase 2 Code Lab: Python, Data, and Machine Learning M5–M9  ·  Details ▼

Python is one of the most widely used languages in AI. You will learn it through practical examples, real datasets, and guided exercises. No prior coding experience is assumed.

→ Python fundamentals with AI-assisted debugging

→ Loading, cleaning, and exploring real-world datasets

→ Statistics that support machine learning decisions

→ Training, testing, and evaluating a supervised learning model

→ Building your first machine learning model

By the end of Phase 2: You will have a working ML model and enough Python confidence to keep building.

Phase 3 Modern AI: Applications in the Real World M10–M14  ·  Details ▼

This phase focuses on the AI systems people interact with today: image recognition, language models, generative AI, prompting, and agents. You will learn how they work, how to use them well, and how to evaluate their outputs critically.

→ Computer vision: how machines interpret images

→ Language AI: classification, tone, and ambiguity

→ Large language models: strengths, limits, and hallucinations

→ Prompting: design, testing, and improvement

→ Agentic AI: goals, tools, memory, and guardrails

By the end of Phase 3: You will know how to use modern AI tools thoughtfully, professionally, and responsibly.

Phase 4 Launch Pad: Build and Showcase M15–M18  ·  Details ▼

The final phase brings everything together. You will design, build, test, and present a working AI-powered web app. The goal is not just to make something impressive, but to explain how and why it works.

→ Vibe coding: AI-assisted development from idea to prototype

→ Full-stack AI project planning: architecture, user flow, and system design

→ Guided build: integrate AI, handle errors, and test rigorously

→ Showcase: present your web app and explain your design choices

By the end of Phase 4: You will have a portfolio-ready AI web app and the confidence to explain how you built it.

The Structure

Every module follows the same rhythm.

Learn the pattern once. Across all 18 modules, you will always know where you are, what to complete, and what comes next.

1

Read and watch

Concise readings and short micro-videos. One concept at a time, with checks that keep learning active.
2

Complete the activity

Each lesson includes a hands-on task: sorting exercises, AI audits, prompt challenges, hallucination hunts, and real-world applications.
3

Check your understanding

Short knowledge checks help you confirm what you understand and what you may need to revisit.
4

Reflect and discuss

Discussion prompts connect each lesson to real-world questions, so learning goes beyond definitions.
5

Save your work

Each module adds one artifact to your portfolio, creating a visible record of your growth from first lesson to final project.

Progress and Portfolio

Designed to reward growth over time.

The course is designed so that early modules build confidence and later modules give you more room to show what you can create. Your progress matters, and your portfolio becomes stronger as your skills develop.

Before You Begin

Common questions, answered directly.

Do I need prior coding experience? ▼

No. The first four modules contain no code. You will write your first line of Python in Module 5. Learners who have coded before may move faster, but the course is designed so beginners can build confidence step by step.

Am I permitted to use AI tools to assist with assignments? ▼

Yes. You are expected to use AI tools thoughtfully. The goal is to use AI to support learning, improve your work, test ideas, and debug problems — not to bypass thinking. You should always be able to explain what you submit.

How long should I expect each module to take? ▼

Plan for approximately 4 to 5 hours per module. Phase 1 modules may feel lighter. Phase 4 build modules may take longer depending on your project idea. The course is self-paced, so consistency matters more than speed.

What software do I need to install? ▼

Nothing. The course uses browser-based tools, including Google Colab for Python, no-code AI tools for early modules, and AI-assisted development platforms for the final build. A reliable internet connection is the main requirement.

What will this course prepare me for? ▼

This course gives you a practical foundation in AI: enough to understand key concepts, use AI tools effectively, evaluate outputs critically, and build a working AI-powered project. These skills are useful whether you continue in technology, science, business, design, medicine, law, or another field.

What We Expect

Three expectations. Nothing more.

One

Stay curious.

Two
