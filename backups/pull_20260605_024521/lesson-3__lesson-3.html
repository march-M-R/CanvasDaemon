Core Idea

AI often works quietly in the background.

Most people do not experience AI as a robot. They experience it as a suggestion, prediction, filter, ranking, caption, or recognition system.

Modern AI is embedded into products people already use.

Open: AI or just automation?

A tool is more likely to involve AI when it handles variation, uses patterns, ranks options, predicts outcomes, recognises input, or adapts based on data.

A simple timer or calculator may be useful, but it usually follows fixed rules.
