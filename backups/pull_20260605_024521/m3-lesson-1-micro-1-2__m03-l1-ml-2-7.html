What Training Samples Really Mean

Examples are not magic. They are evidence the model uses.

Big Question

“Why does the model only know what it has seen before?”

Concept Companion

Volume, variety, balance.

Three properties of your sample set decide what the model can do. Get them right and a small dataset is enough. Get them wrong and a large dataset is wasted.

1

How many

Volume

30+ samples per class is the floor for a working demo. Fewer is unreliable.

2

How different

Variety

Different angles, lighting, backgrounds, distances. The variety is the lesson.

3

How even

Balance

Roughly equal counts per class. Imbalance creates a 'lazy default' shortcut.

Key Insight

The model is its examples.

A machine learning model is not a brain. It is a function whose only knowledge comes from the labeled examples you fed it. Change the examples and you change the model.

Compare

30 samples vs 30 samples.

Both training sets have 30 samples. Only one of them teaches the model anything useful. The difference is variety.

Set

Narrow

Photos taken

30 photos at one desk, one light

What the model learns

How to recognize this desk under this light

Set

Varied

Photos taken

30 photos across angles, lights, backgrounds

What the model learns

What the subject actually looks like

Set

Imbalanced

Photos taken

60 of one class, 10 of the other

What the model learns

To always guess the bigger class

Apply

Play: Sample Stack

Open your phone's photo album. Look at the most recent 30 photos. How many distinct conditions are represented? That is the kind of variety your training set needs.
