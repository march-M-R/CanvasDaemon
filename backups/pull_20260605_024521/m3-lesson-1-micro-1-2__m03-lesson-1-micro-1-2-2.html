What 'Training Samples' Really Means

Concept Companion

Samples are the model's only experience.

A model has no common sense. Every belief it has about a class comes from the samples you fed it. Twenty boring, similar samples teach a narrow lesson. Thirty varied samples teach a real one.

One labeled example

Sample

A single photo, clip, or row tagged with the class it belongs to.

How many you need

Volume

At least 30 per class for a working demo. More for harder problems.

What makes them useful

Variety

Different angles, lighting, distances, and backgrounds within each class.

Real-World Case

Volume alone is not enough

In 2020, a published COVID X-ray classifier claimed 98% accuracy. When researchers tested it on a different hospital's scans, accuracy collapsed. Why? The 'hundreds of samples' all came from one source. Volume without variety teaches the model the source, not the disease.

Apply

Try it now.

Look at your bedroom. If you had to teach a model to recognize 'desk' versus 'bed', what twelve photos would you take to cover real variety?
