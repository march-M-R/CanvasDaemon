Before You Watch

Proxies do the work the protected attribute used to.

Even when a model never sees race, gender, or age, it can use zip code, school name, or browsing history as a stand-in. The result is a system that looks neutral on paper and produces unequal outcomes in practice.

Concept Companion

Three ways disparate impact appears.

Removing a sensitive attribute from the dataset does not remove the pattern. The model finds it elsewhere.

Stand-in

Proxy variable

Zip code, school name, browsing history. Each carries protected attribute information.

Unequal results

Disparate impact

One group is approved, hired, or flagged at very different rates.

Bias grows

Feedback loop

Biased outcomes generate biased training data for the next model.
