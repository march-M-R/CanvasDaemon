2.3 Why AI Took Off Now

The Four Factors

The “perfect storm” behind modern AI

1. More Data

The internet, smartphones, social media, video platforms, sensors, and digital records created massive amounts of usable data.

2. More Compute

Cloud infrastructure and specialised chips made it possible to train and deploy much larger models.

3. Better Algorithms

Researchers developed more effective machine learning and deep learning techniques that could use the data and compute more efficiently.

4. Connected Platforms

Apps, APIs, and cloud services made AI easy to distribute to millions of users quickly.
