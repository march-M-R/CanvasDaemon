Course Roadmap

Four phases. One complete picture of AI.

This course begins with everyday intuition, then moves into Python, machine learning, modern AI tools, and a final portfolio-ready AI project.

Foundations  ·  Code Lab  ·  Modern AI  ·  Launch Pad

The roadmap is designed as a gradual learning journey. Each phase builds on the previous one, helping you move from understanding AI concepts to building and presenting your own AI-powered application.

Explore the Phases

What you will learn in each phase

Phase 1 Foundations: Concepts and Intuition M1 to M4 · Open ▼

You begin with the question everyone should be able to answer clearly: what does artificial intelligence actually mean? By the end of Phase 1, you will have a working vocabulary, a critical lens, and enough intuition to recognise AI in everyday life.

What is artificial intelligence, and what is just automation?

How do machines learn from data?

How can you build and test a classifier with no code?

What do bias, fairness, privacy, and ethics look like in AI?

By the end of Phase 1: You will be able to explain the basics of AI clearly, without relying on buzzwords.

Phase 2 Code Lab: Python, Data, and Machine Learning M5 to M9 · Open ▼

Python is one of the most widely used languages in AI. You will learn it through practical examples, real datasets, and guided exercises. No prior coding experience is assumed.

Python fundamentals with AI-assisted debugging

Loading, cleaning, and exploring real-world datasets

Statistics that support machine learning decisions

Training, testing, and evaluating a supervised learning model

Building your first machine learning model

By the end of Phase 2: You will have a working ML model and enough Python confidence to keep building.

Phase 3 Modern AI: Applications in the Real World M10 to M14 · Open ▼

This phase focuses on the AI systems people interact with today: image recognition, language models, generative AI, prompting, and agents. You will learn how they work, how to use them well, and how to evaluate their outputs critically.

Computer vision: how machines interpret images

Language AI: classification, tone, and ambiguity

Large language models: strengths, limits, and hallucinations

Prompting: design, testing, and improvement

Agentic AI: goals, tools, memory, and guardrails

By the end of Phase 3: You will know how to use modern AI tools thoughtfully, professionally, and responsibly.

Phase 4 Launch Pad: Build and Showcase M15 to M18 · Open ▼

The final phase brings everything together. You will design, build, test, and present a working AI-powered web app. The goal is not just to make something impressive, but to explain how and why it works.

Vibe coding: AI-assisted development from idea to prototype

Full-stack AI project planning: architecture, user flow, and system design

Guided build: integrate AI, handle errors, and test rigorously

Showcase: present your web app and explain your design choices

By the end of Phase 4: You will have a portfolio-ready AI web app and the confidence to explain how you built it.
