By the end of this module, you should be able to explain what AI is, where it appears, and why its outputs need careful evaluation.

Module Mindset

Notice what is already around you.

You will look at familiar systems such as recommendations, maps, chatbots, captions, search, and face unlock. The goal is to separate ordinary software from systems that learn from patterns.

Learning Outcomes

What you will be able to do

Outcome 1

Define artificial intelligence and distinguish it from automation, ordinary software, machine learning, and generative AI.

Outcome 2

Explain how data, labels, training, testing, and feedback shape the behavior of AI systems.

Outcome 3

Identify AI in everyday tools and explain how it influences digital experiences and decisions.

Outcome 4

Evaluate AI systems for bias, reliability, hallucination, privacy, and the need for human oversight.

Outcome 5

Communicate AI concepts and limitations clearly to technical and nontechnical audiences.

Module Goal

Build a practical vocabulary for recognizing and questioning AI in real life.

Module Roadmap
