Before You Watch

Magic is just a function with good inputs.

“Recommenders feel surprising because the inputs are invisible and the outputs are personalized. Strip away the marketing and you find the same shape every time: structured inputs in, ranked outputs back.”

Concept Companion

Inputs, model, outputs.

Every AI feature in any product uses the same shape. Knowing what each of the three pieces is for a given feature is the start of reading any system.

What goes in

Inputs

Behavior history, item features, context. Everything the app has on file about you and the items it can recommend.

What sits in the middle

Model

A function that maps inputs to scores. The 'AI' part. Could be a simple linear model or a deep network.

What comes back

Outputs

Usually a ranked list. The app picks the top few to show. The ones below the cut are invisible to you.

Compare

Same shape, three apps.

Every recommender, in every product, has the same shape. The inputs and outputs change. The shape does not.

Music streaming

Inputs

Listen history, song features, time of day

Outputs

Next 50 songs, ranked

Maps

Inputs

Origin, destination, traffic, your route history

Outputs

Three or four route options, ranked by ETA

Email autocomplete

Inputs

Recent words you typed, common phrases

Outputs

Three suggested next words

Shopping app

Inputs

Browse history, item features, price tier

Outputs

Ranked product list on home screen
