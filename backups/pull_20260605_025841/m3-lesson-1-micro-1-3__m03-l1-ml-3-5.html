Big Question

Before You Watch

Variety beats quantity.

Twenty samples taken across very different conditions teach the model more than fifty samples taken in one moment under one light. The variety is the lesson the model takes home.

Concept Companion

What to vary deliberately.

Three knobs decide whether your dataset teaches the model the subject or teaches it the photo conditions. Vary all three on purpose.

Move around your subject

Angle

Top, side, 45 degrees, close, far. Every angle is a new lesson.

Change the light source

Lighting

Window, ceiling, dim, bright, mixed. The pixels change. The model only sees pixels.

Vary the scene behind

Background

Don't let the background be the strongest signal. Move the subject around.

Compare

Narrow capture vs deliberate variety.

Two students. Same class problem. Same one hour of capture time. Very different models.

ChoiceCapture sessionResult on Day 3 testing
Lazy30 photos at the same deskWorks at the desk. Fails everywhere else.
Deliberate30 photos across 5 locations and 3 light sourcesGeneralizes to new spaces, mostly.
Lazy with volume100 photos at the same deskStill fails everywhere else. Just more confidently.

Apply

Play: Photo Scout

Before you train tomorrow, write down five conditions you will deliberately vary across your samples. Specific. Different angle. Different light. Different background. Different distance. Different framing.
