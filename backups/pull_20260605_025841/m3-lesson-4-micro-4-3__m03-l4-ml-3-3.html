The Diminishing Returns Curve

Adding 500 more samples often does not double accuracy.

Big Question

When does more data stop helping?
Watch → Read → Apply

Before You Watch

Early samples are the most valuable.

Going from 5 to 30 samples per class transforms a model. Going from 30 to 300 helps less. Going from 300 to 3000 helps less still. The curve flattens. Where you are on it changes what to do next.

Concept Companion

Three zones. Three strategies.

Where you sit on the curve decides whether the next hour should be raw capture, targeted edge cases, or moving on entirely.

5 → 30 samples

Steep zone

Every sample dramatically changes the model. Just keep capturing.

30 → 100 samples

Helpful zone

Real gains, especially with variety. Quality starts to matter more than quantity.

100+ samples

Flat zone

Volume barely helps. Only targeted edge case samples move the needle.
