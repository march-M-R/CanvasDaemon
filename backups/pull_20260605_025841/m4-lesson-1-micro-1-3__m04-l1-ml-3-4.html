Before You Watch

Costs land on the person, not the system.

“When an AI denies a loan, flags a resume, or mis-tags a photo, the affected person carries the cost. The team that built the model rarely meets them. Designing AI responsibly means thinking about this cost flow.”

Concept Companion

Three kinds of cost.

Not all costs are visible immediately. Some compound. Some never get measured.

Direct harm

Visible

Lost job, denied service, false accusation. The cost that makes the news.

Wear and tear

Hidden

Time spent appealing, emotional weight, lost trust. Rarely reported.

Spreads

Compounding

One flag may follow someone across multiple systems and stay even after a fix.

Compare

Same mistake, different stories.

Two stakeholders. One AI mistake. Very different cost experiences.

The team that built the model

What they experience

A bug report, maybe a press cycle

How long it lasts

Resolved when the patch ships

The affected person

What they experience

Lost opportunity, time spent appealing, residual record

How long it lasts

Often permanent or hard to undo

Downstream users

What they experience

Reduced trust in similar systems

How long it lasts

Affects every future AI deployment
