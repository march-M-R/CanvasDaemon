Reading a Privacy Policy in 90 Seconds

Skip the legalese. Find three questions, three answers.

Big Question

What do you need to know before agreeing to a privacy policy?
Watch → Read → Apply

Before You Watch

Three questions cover most of it.

Privacy policies are long on purpose. You do not need to read every line. You need three answers: what data is collected, who sees it, and how long it is kept. Each one has a search term that jumps you to the right section.

Concept Companion

The three searches.

These three search terms cut through any privacy policy in under two minutes. They are not perfect. They catch the headlines.

What

'Collect'

Jumps to the data list: location, contacts, microphone, photos, browsing, biometrics.

Who

'Share'

Jumps to the sharing list: partners, advertisers, vendors, governments. Watch for vague language.

How long

'Retain'

Jumps to retention: a specific period, 'as long as active,' or 'indefinitely.' The last one is the warning sign.

Compare

How different policies handle the three questions.

Three real-world patterns. The third one is the warning sign.

PatternWhat it usually meansHow concerning
Specific list of fields, specific list of partnersHonest. Probably annoying to write. Worth respectingLow
'We may share with partners and affiliates'Anything goes. The vagueness is doing workMedium-high

Apply

Play: 90-Second Privacy Read

Open one app on your phone right now. Find the privacy policy. Use Find or Ctrl+F to search for 'collect,' 'share,' and 'retain.' Write three bullets. That is your 90-second policy read.
