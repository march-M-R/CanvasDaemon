Core Idea

Privacy is who can connect data back to you.

Anonymous data is rarely fully anonymous once it is combined with other datasets. Privacy is not a single label. It is a chain of choices about what is collected, who sees it, and how it can be linked.

Key Takeaways

What you should remember

Stripping a name from a record does not always make it anonymous.

Most privacy policies are written to be agreed to, not understood.

Real consent requires that the person knows what they are agreeing to and can actually say no.
