The 'Human in the Loop' Explained

Three patterns. Three different oversight intensities.

Big Question

What does 'human in the loop' actually mean in practice?
Watch → Read → Apply

Before You Watch

Three patterns. Pick one on purpose.

Human-in-the-loop is not one design. It is a spectrum: a human approves every decision, a human samples decisions, or a human only reviews when the model is uncertain. Each is appropriate for different contexts.

Concept Companion

Three patterns of human oversight.

The choice depends on stakes, volume, and how well the model knows what it does not know.

Highest oversight

Every decision

A human signs off on every output. Slow. Used in safety contexts.

Medium oversight

Sampled review

A human spot-checks a percentage. Catches drift over time.

Lightest oversight

Triggered review

A human only sees decisions the model flags as uncertain.

Compare

When each level is appropriate.

Different contexts demand different oversight levels. The choice should be explicit, not accidental.

DomainVolume of decisionsBest oversight level
Medical diagnosis supportHundreds per day per clinicianEvery decision
Content moderation at scaleMillions per dayTriggered review, with sampling
Loan approvalThousands per dayEvery decision (legally required for adverse actions)
Photo auto-taggingBillions per daySampled review for drift
