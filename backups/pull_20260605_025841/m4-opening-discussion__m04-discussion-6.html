MODULE 4 · OPENING DISCUSSION

Whose fairness wins?

Before we audit AI, defend a definition.

5 Points · Graded
Definitions. Trade-offs. Justice.

Three fairness definitions. They conflict. Pick yours.

The Scenario

A high school district plans to deploy an AI tool to flag students "at risk of dropping out." Three teachers, with different priorities, weigh in.

Which definition of fairness should the district pick, and what are they accepting in trade-off?

Think about:

• Group fairness: Equalize flag rates across demographic groups. What is sacrificed?
• Individual fairness: Similar students get similar treatment. What is sacrificed?
• Procedural fairness: Students and families can see and appeal. What is sacrificed?

Your Task

  1. Initial post (3 to 5 sentences): Pick one fairness definition. Defend it. Name what you are giving up.
  2. Reply to TWO classmates (2 to 3 sentences each): Push them on the trade-off they accepted. What real harm does it cause?
  3. Be specific. Name a specific group affected by your choice and what they would experience.
