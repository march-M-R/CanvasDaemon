Ethics is not a separate topic from technical work. It is part of every decision: what data you collect, who is affected, when a human must be in the loop.

Module Hook

If an AI tool gives an answer confidently, does that mean the answer is safe or fair?

This module gives you the tools and language to answer that question yourself by doing the work and documenting honestly what you find.

Learning Outcomes

What you will be able to do

Outcome 1

Define algorithmic fairness and identify disparate impact in real AI examples.

Outcome 2

Read a privacy policy and summarize its data practices in three honest bullets.

Outcome 3

Explain why large language models hallucinate and recognize confident-but-wrong outputs.

Outcome 4

Decide when a human-in-the-loop review is required versus optional.

Outcome 5

Run a structured AI risk review on a real model and document harms, affected groups, and mitigations.

Module Goal

Be able to spot fairness, privacy, hallucination, and oversight risks in a real AI system and propose concrete fixes.

Module Roadmap

Your learning journey

Each lesson builds on the previous one. Complete them in order for the smoothest path through the module.

Fairness and Unequal Impact

Hallucinations and Bias

Human Oversight and Risk Review

Decide when humans must review, and run a structured risk review.

5

Synthesis and Showcase

Submit a completed risk review and an AI Risk Postmortem to your portfolio.
