Vision AI vs Speech AI

Type Input Everyday example
Vision AI Images or video frames Face unlock, photo search, object detection
Speech AI Audio or spoken language Captions, voice assistants, transcription
Open: Why recognition AI can make mistakes

Recognition systems depend on training examples. If the data is incomplete, biased, low quality, or different from real-world conditions, the system may misrecognise people, objects, accents, sounds, or context.
