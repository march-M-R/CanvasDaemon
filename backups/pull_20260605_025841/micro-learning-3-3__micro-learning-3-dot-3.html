Prediction AI

Prediction systems estimate what is likely to happen next, what category something belongs to, or which option may be best.

Big Question

How does AI make predictions about the future or the next best action?
Watch → Predict → Evaluate

Before You Watch

Think about maps.

When a map app estimates arrival time, it is not seeing the future. It is using patterns from traffic, distance, speed, time of day, and past data.

What makes one prediction more reliable than another?

Open a hint

A prediction is stronger when the system has useful data, relevant patterns, and a situation that is similar to what it has seen before.

Examples

Common prediction systems

System What it predicts Input signals
Maps Arrival time or best route Distance, traffic, road speed, time of day, location
Fraud detection Suspicious or normal transaction Amount, merchant, location, device, spending history
Typing suggestions Next word or phrase Previous words, common phrases, writing context
Weather apps Chance of rain, storm risk, temperature Sensor data, satellite data, historical weather patterns

Key idea: Prediction AI does not know the future. It estimates what is likely based on patterns and available evidence.

Infographic

Prediction is not the same as certainty

Probability

Many predictions are estimates, not guarantees.

Confidence

A system may be more or less confident depending on the situation.

Consequence

A wrong prediction matters more in high-stakes decisions.

Example: A wrong movie recommendation is low-stakes. A wrong fraud, medical, or safety prediction can have serious consequences.

Open: Prediction is not certainty

AI predictions are estimates based on patterns. They can be useful, but they are not guaranteed.

A prediction can be wrong if the data is incomplete, the situation changes, the pattern does not apply, or the system was trained on examples that do not represent the current case well.

Meme-Level Summary

“The map said 12 minutes… and then traffic entered the chat.”

Prediction AI can be helpful, but the real world can change after the prediction is made.
