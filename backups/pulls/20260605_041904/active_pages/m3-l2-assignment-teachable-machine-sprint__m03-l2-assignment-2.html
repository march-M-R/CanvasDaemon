Project Brief

Train your own classifier in Teachable Machine.

In this activity you will use Google's Teachable Machine (free, browser-based, no account required) to train a real two-class image classifier. You will capture your own samples, train the model, test it, and submit a one-page summary. You do not need to write any code.

Time required: Roughly 45 to 60 minutes from start to finish. Most of that is capture time and waiting for the model to train.

Pick a two-class problem

Step 1: Choose two classes worth distinguishing.

Your problem must pass three tests from Lesson 1:

Distinct

A stranger looking at 5 random samples from each class could sort them with no training.

Capturable

You can capture 30+ varied samples per class today with your phone or webcam.

Useful

Name one real person who benefits when the model is right.

Examples that work: ripe vs unripe avocados, paper vs plastic bottle, left vs right shoe, cat vs dog photos. Examples that struggle: cucumber vs zucchini (too similar), mood vs mood (too subjective).

Run the sprint

Steps 2 through 5: Capture, train, test, save.

One-page summary

Write a short reflection.

In a single page (or one Canvas text-entry box), answer these four questions in 2 to 3 sentences each:

  1. What is your two-class problem and who benefits when it is right?
  2. How did you deliberately vary your samples? (angle, lighting, background, distance, framing)
  3. Where did the model fail in testing, and what does that tell you about its training data?
  4. What is one thing you would change before trusting this model in a real product?

Submission checklist
