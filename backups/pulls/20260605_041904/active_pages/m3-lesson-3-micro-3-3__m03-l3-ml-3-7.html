Reading Confidence Scores

High confidence is not the same as correct.

Big Question

“What does it mean when a model is 99% confident and wrong?”

Concept Companion

Four combinations. Different responses.

Confidence and correctness are two separate axes. Reading both together is the only way to act on a prediction responsibly.

1

The good case

High and correct

Model knows. Treat as a normal good prediction.

2

The dangerous case

High and wrong

Confidently incorrect. Points to a shortcut the model is exploiting.

3

Lucky or borderline

Low and correct

Right answer with little certainty. Worth understanding.

Key Insight

Confidence is belief. Not truth.

A model can be confidently wrong. A model can also be correct but unsure. Treat confidence as one signal alongside the label, not the final answer.
