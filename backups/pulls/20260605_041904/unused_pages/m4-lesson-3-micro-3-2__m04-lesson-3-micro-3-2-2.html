Confident and Correct Are Not the Same

Concept Companion

Confident answers shut down questions.

When a model writes with certainty, people stop double-checking. That is exactly when verification matters most.

Useful

Confident and right

Verify anyway for important decisions.

Dangerous

Confident and wrong

Looks like an authoritative source and is not.

Honest

Hedged and right

The model signals uncertainty, which is often more helpful.

Apply

Try it now.

Find one confident LLM answer you used recently. Check it against an independent source. Were you right to trust it?
