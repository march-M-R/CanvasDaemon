CameraVision AI
What it does
Recognises faces, scenes, objects, and text in real time as you point the camera.
Input
Pixel data from the camera sensor, processed as image arrays.
AI in action
Face detection for portrait mode, scene recognition to adjust exposure, object detection for suggested tags.
Not AI
The shutter button, zoom controls, and saving to storage are conventional software.
♪Music StreamingRecommendation AI
What it does
Selects and ranks songs you are likely to enjoy based on your listening history and the habits of similar users.
Input
Play events, skip events, replay events, save events, and listening duration across millions of users.
AI in action
Discover Weekly is generated weekly using collaborative filtering. Every user gets a different playlist.
Not AI
Playback controls, volume, and searching for a specific song by name are conventional software.
AIVoice AssistantLanguage AI
What it does
Converts speech to text, understands the intent behind your request, and generates or retrieves an appropriate response.
Input
Audio waveforms converted to text, then processed as natural language.
AI in action
Speech recognition converts your voice to text. Intent classification decides what you want. Response generation produces the answer.
Not AI
Setting an alarm for a specific time, once understood, is carried out by conventional software.
MapsPredictive AI
What it does
Estimates travel time, predicts traffic conditions, and routes you around delays before they fully form.
Input
GPS signals from millions of devices, historical traffic patterns by time of day and day of week, incident reports.
AI in action
Travel time prediction uses historical and live data. Route optimisation uses learned traffic patterns. Incident detection uses anomalies in GPS speed.
Not AI
Drawing the map, calculating straight-line distance, and satellite imagery rendering are conventional software.
Short VideoRecommendation AI
What it does
Selects the next video you see based on what you have watched, rewatched, commented on, shared, or scrolled past.
Input
Watch time, replay rate, comment activity, share behaviour, follows, and the content of videos you engage with.
AI in action
The For You Page is rebuilt constantly. A video shown to a small group first. Strong engagement signals wider distribution. Your feed is different from every other user's.
Not AI
Playback, volume, upload, and direct messaging are conventional software.
+Health TrackingPredictive AI
What it does
Detects irregular heart rhythms, predicts sleep quality, and identifies unusual patterns in biometric data that may indicate health changes.
Input
Heart rate sensor data, accelerometer readings, GPS movement patterns, sleep motion data.
AI in action
Atrial fibrillation detection uses an ECG model. Sleep stage prediction uses motion and heart rate patterns. Trend analysis flags unusual changes over time.
Not AI
Counting steps by counting accelerometer peaks is conventional sensor logic, not AI.
MessagesLanguage AI
What it does
Predicts the next word you are likely to type, corrects spelling in context, and suggests quick reply options.
Input
The words you have typed in this message, your typing history, and general language patterns from training data.
AI in action
Predictive text uses a language model trained on your writing style. Contextual autocorrect uses the surrounding words, not just the misspelled word. Smart replies generate short suggested responses.
Not AI
Sending, receiving, storing, and encrypting messages are conventional software.
Photo LibraryVision AI
What it does
Automatically organises your photos by face, place, object, and scene without you doing anything.
Input
Raw image pixel data from every photo in your library, processed locally on your device.
AI in action
Face clustering groups photos by person. Scene recognition identifies beach, mountain, food, and more. Text in image detection makes photos searchable by words they contain.
Not AI
Sorting by date, storing files, and sharing photos are conventional software.
