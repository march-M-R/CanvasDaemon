Portfolio Deliverable · Module 2

Build Your Data System Diagram

Pick a real AI system (one you use, one in the news, or one you wish existed). Document its data, features, labels, and pipeline. When you finish, download the one-page system diagram and save it to your portfolio.

Step 1

Name your system

Pick something specific. Not "AI." Not "ChatGPT." Pick a single feature in a single product.

Step 2

Identify your data

What does the system collect? Where does it come from?

List as many as you can think of, comma separated.
What does the user actually see?
Step 3

List three strong features

Features are the columns the model actually uses. Pick three that you think carry real signal, and explain why.

Engineered means you transformed raw data (e.g., "hour of day" derived from a timestamp).
Step 4

Where do the labels come from?

If the model is supervised, who or what labels the training data? If unsupervised, write "no labels."

A bias, a gap, or a way the label might mislead the model.
Step 5

One known weakness

If this system was wrong, who would carry the cost? Be specific.
