Capturing Good Training Data

Concept Companion

Diversity beats quantity.

Thirty samples taken from one angle, in one room, with one light source, will look identical to the model. Twenty samples taken across different conditions teach the model the actual shape of the class.

Move around your subject

Angle

Capture from above, below, sides, near, and far.

Change the light

Lighting

Window light, ceiling light, dim light, bright light, mixed light.

Vary the scene

Background

A model that only sees one background will learn the background, not the subject.

Apply

Try it now.

Before you train anything, write down five things you will deliberately vary across your samples. This is your data plan.
