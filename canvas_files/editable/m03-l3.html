Core Idea

If you only test on training data, you have not tested.

A model that scores 100 percent on its training data could still fail on anything new. Real testing uses fresh samples the model has never seen.

Key Takeaways

What you should remember

Test on at least 10 fresh samples per class, none of them from training.

Log every prediction with its confidence score, even the ones the model gets right.

A confusion matrix tells you which class the model confuses with which.
