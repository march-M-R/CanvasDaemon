You will build a working image classifier with no code, then stress-test it until you can describe exactly where it works and where it fails.

Module Hook

Can you train a machine to tell two things apart in under an hour, and would you trust it?

This module gives you the tools and language to answer that question yourself by doing the work and documenting honestly what you find.

Learning Outcomes

What you will be able to do

Outcome 1

Build a working 2-class image classifier in Teachable Machine with at least 30 balanced samples per class.

Outcome 2

Diagnose model mistakes using a confusion matrix, false positives, false negatives, and confidence scores.

Outcome 3

Iterate on a model by adding targeted training data to fix its most common failure modes.

Outcome 4

Recognize the limits of a 'demo-ready' classifier and describe edge cases where it should not be trusted.

Outcome 5

Document your model in a one-page Model Card that a non-technical reader could understand.

Module Goal

Ship a trained 2-class image classifier and a one-page Model Card that honestly describes when to trust it.

Module Roadmap

Your learning journey

Each lesson builds on the previous one. Complete them in order for the smoothest path through the module.

1

Sprint Brief

Frame the build, lock in your 2-class problem, and learn what classification really means.

2

Setup and Training

Build classes in Teachable Machine, capture balanced samples, train, and save the model.

3

Testing and Mistakes

Test on fresh samples, log predictions, read confidence, and analyze your confusion matrix.

4

Iteration and Limits

Patch the weakest area, retest, then break the model on purpose to find its real limits.

5

Ship and Showcase

Submit the classifier link, your test log, and a finished Model Card to your portfolio.
