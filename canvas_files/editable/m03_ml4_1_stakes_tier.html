Stakes Tier

"Good enough" depends entirely on what happens when the model is wrong. Place each AI use case into the stakes tier where it actually belongs. The tier determines the accuracy bar and the level of oversight required.

Low Stakes

Personal use, hobby, demo
Bar: 80-90% accuracy is fine. No human review required.

Medium Stakes

Customer feature, social product
Bar: Higher accuracy plus "I don't know" fallback. Sampled human review.

High Stakes

Safety, health, legal, financial
Bar: Very high accuracy plus mandatory human review of every decision.
