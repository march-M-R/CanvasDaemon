Model Card Builder

A Model Card is a one-page honest description of what your AI does, where it works, and where it should not be trusted. Fill in each step. Watch the card take shape on the right. Download when you're done and save it to your portfolio.

Build your card, step by step

Each section asks one specific thing. Aim for one or two clean sentences per box.

Honest beats impressive. A card that names a real weakness is worth more than a card that hides one.

1Model name & purpose

Two sentences max. What does the model do, and who would use it?

Example: "Classifies a phone photo of an avocado as 'ripe' or 'overripe'. Built for shoppers who have trouble assessing fruit by sight."

2Your two classes

Name each class and write one sentence about how you defined it. Edge cases matter.

3Training data summary

How many samples per class? What did you deliberately vary?

4Performance on fresh test samples

Numbers from your test log. The model never saw these photos during training.

5Known weaknesses

Be honest. What lighting, angles, or backgrounds break it?

6Intended use & out-of-scope use

Where would you use this? Where should it absolutely not be used?

7Trust statement

One sentence each. This is the hardest part. Be specific.
