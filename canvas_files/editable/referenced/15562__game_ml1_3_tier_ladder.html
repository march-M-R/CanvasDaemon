Email

A spam filter trained on 500 million emails routes each message to inbox or spam. It cannot write emails or perform any other task.

Note: One task, done extremely well, with no transfer to anything else.

Correct
A spam filter does one thing: classify emails as spam or not spam. It cannot write emails, summarise them, or do anything outside that task. No matter how accurate it is, it is locked to one domain. That is the definition of Narrow AI.
Not quite
General AI describes a system that can reason across many different domains the way a human can. This filter cannot do anything outside spam classification. It cannot transfer its skills to any other area. That is Narrow AI, not General.
Not quite
Superintelligent AI is a theoretical concept referring to a system that exceeds human capability across all domains. A spam filter, however accurate, is extremely far from that. It performs one simple classification task. That is Narrow AI.

Language

A large language model that writes essays, answers questions, summarises documents, and translates between languages. It cannot reason reliably about its own limitations or generalise beyond language-based tasks.

Note: Impressive breadth within language, but still constrained to one domain type.

Correct
Despite its impressive breadth within language tasks, this is still Narrow AI. It operates within one domain and cannot transfer to physical tasks, novel real-world reasoning, or skills it was not trained on. Breadth within a single domain is not the same as General AI.
Worth discussing
Many researchers debate this, and it is a fair question. But most still classify current large language models as Narrow AI because they cannot reason across all domains the way a human can. The debate is real and ongoing, but the majority view places them at Tier 1.
Not quite
Superintelligent AI would exceed human capability across everything. A language model, however capable within language, does not come close to that description. It still fails on tasks outside its training domain.

Biochemistry

A system that predicts the 3D structure of proteins from their amino acid sequences with extraordinary accuracy. It cannot analyse medical images, write code, or perform any task outside protein structure prediction.

Note: Scientific impact does not equal breadth of capability.

Correct
The scientific impact of this system is enormous, but its capability is locked entirely to protein structure prediction. It cannot transfer any of its ability to any other domain. A system can be transformative in one field and still be Narrow AI. Impact and breadth are different things.
Not quite
General AI implies cross-domain reasoning. This system cannot do anything outside protein structures. Exceptional performance within one specialised scientific domain is still Narrow AI, not General AI.
Not quite
Superintelligent AI would exceed human intelligence across all domains. This system is superhuman within one narrow scientific task. That is Narrow AI with exceptional capability in a very specific area, not superintelligence.

Hypothetical

A described system that can learn any intellectual task a human can, transfer knowledge from one domain to another without retraining, handle completely novel situations, and explain its own reasoning accurately.

Note: This is a description, not a real product. Place it based on what the description implies, not on any specific tool name.

Not quite
Narrow AI is locked to one task or domain. This description explicitly includes cross-domain transfer and the ability to handle novel situations. That goes beyond what any Narrow AI system can do. Place it on the next tier.
Correct
This description matches the definition of General AI: human-level performance across intellectual tasks, genuine cross-domain transfer, and the ability to handle situations it was not explicitly trained on. This system does not currently exist, but the description fits Tier 2 in the framework.
Close, but not quite
Superintelligent AI would surpass human capability. This description says human-level, meaning it matches human performance but does not exceed it across all domains. That distinction matters. General AI is the better fit here.

Gaming

A chess-playing AI that has defeated every human world champion it has faced. When asked to play a different board game with slightly different rules, it fails completely and requires retraining from scratch.

Note: The word superhuman appears in descriptions of this system. Does superhuman performance change its tier?

Correct
Superhuman performance within one domain is still Narrow AI. This system cannot transfer any chess knowledge to any other task. The moment the rules change, it fails entirely. Tier 1 can include superhuman performance within its narrow domain. The tier is about breadth, not skill level.
Not quite
General AI requires cross-domain reasoning. This system cannot do anything outside chess. Superhuman within one game is still Narrow. The key test is transfer: can it apply its knowledge elsewhere? It cannot.
Not quite
Superintelligence implies exceeding human capability across all domains. Defeating humans at chess while being unable to do anything else is the opposite of that. Narrow AI is the correct tier.

Agentic Systems

A system that uses tools such as a web browser, calendar, and code editor to complete multi-step tasks. Given a goal like "book a meeting with three people next week," it searches calendars, drafts messages, and confirms bookings. It fails on tasks outside its designed workflow.

Note: Multi-step task completion does not automatically mean General AI. Consider what the system can and cannot do.

Correct
Agentic systems that use tools are still Narrow AI. The complexity of the workflow does not change the tier. This system fails outside its designed scope and has no general reasoning ability. It is a more sophisticated Narrow AI, sometimes called an AI agent, but Narrow AI nonetheless.
Not quite
Multi-step capability is impressive, but General AI requires true cross-domain reasoning and transfer. This system fails on tasks outside its workflow. That boundary is the defining feature of Narrow AI. The workflow complexity is not the same as genuine generalisation.
Not quite
An agent that books meetings is a useful and sophisticated tool, but it is very far from exceeding human capability across all domains. Narrow AI is the correct placement on the ladder.

All six cases classified.

The tier that matters is breadth, not performance. Narrow AI can be superhuman within its domain. General AI does not yet exist. Superintelligence is still theoretical. When you see a new AI claim, ask: what can it not do?
