The key lesson

The three hallucinations shared one pattern: they were specific, plausible, contextually appropriate, and tonally identical to the accurate responses around them. There was no hesitation, no qualification, no signal of uncertainty. This is why hallucinations are dangerous and why verification is a necessary habit when working with AI-generated content.
