Module 4 Lesson 2 · Big Activity

Privacy and Consent Audit Workshop

Pick a real app you (or someone in your family) uses. Audit what it collects, who sees it, how long it is kept, and whether real informed consent was given. When you finish, download the one-page Privacy Audit Card and submit it as your Lesson 2 deliverable.

Step 1

Name the app you are auditing

Pick a specific app, not a category. For example, "Instagram" is fine. "Social media" is too broad.

Step 2

What does it collect?

Check each data type the app collects. Tap a tag to toggle it.

Step 3

Who sees the data?

Pick the option closest to what the app's actual privacy policy says (or what its behavior suggests).

Step 4

Was real consent given?

Pick the closest description of how this user agreed to the privacy terms.

Step 5

Name three specific risks

For this app and this user, what are three concrete things that could go wrong with the data being collected, shared, or retained?

Step 6

The consent question

Write the one specific question this user should have asked, in plain language, before installing the app. Not "is this safe?" but something concrete.
