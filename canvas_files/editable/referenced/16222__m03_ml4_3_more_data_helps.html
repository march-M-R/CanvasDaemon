Apply the curve

Three scenarios. For each, pick the move that gets the biggest accuracy gain per hour of work.
Scenario 1
Your model has 8 samples per class. You have one hour. What's the best use of it?
Best answer: Add 30 more samples. You're in the steep zone (8 samples is below the floor). Pure volume buys you the most accuracy from here. Variety matters more later. 100 samples is overkill for this stage and harder to capture in an hour.
Scenario 2
Your model has 50 samples per class. Accuracy stalled at 85%. What's the best move?
Best answer: 10 targeted samples in new conditions. You're in the helpful zone. Adding 200 similar samples teaches the model nothing new. 10 samples covering an angle or lighting it hasn't seen will move the needle far more.
Scenario 3
Your model has 200 samples per class. Accuracy is 91%. The confusion matrix shows it's failing on dim-lit photos. What now?
Best answer: 15-20 targeted dim-lit samples. You're well into the flat zone. Volume buys you almost nothing now. Targeted samples that fix a specific failure are worth 10x what raw volume is worth from here.
