Module Closeout Activity

Complete and save the module evidence

Complete one Agent Workflow Analysis. This is an understanding artifact - not the final capstone specification.

Goal and success

State the goal, intended user, success criteria, and whether the task should be an agent, workflow, assistant, or not built.

Inputs and data

List inputs, data sensitivity, approved sources, and what must never be collected.

Loop and patterns

Show observations, decisions, actions, checks, and the smallest suitable pattern.

Tools and permissions

Name each tool, read/write effect, minimum permission, and reversibility.

Oversight

Place checkpoints and state who reviews what evidence and who remains responsible.

Stopping and recovery

Set success, attempt, uncertainty, escalation, interruption, and recovery rules.

Evaluation

Provide a normal, edge, and adversarial case; score outcome and process.

Final quality test

Can a classmate see what the system may do, what it may not do, what evidence it uses, when a person intervenes, and how it stops safely? If not, revise it.

Bridge forward

Carry this analysis into M15, where you will turn the strongest idea into a clear, build-ready concept. Implementation starts in M16.
