Core Idea

LLMs are powerful prediction systems.

A large language model does not “look up truth” by default. It generates likely text based on patterns learned from large amounts of language data.

Important Limits

Three things to remember

Not a human mind

An LLM does not understand the world exactly like a person.

Not always current

It may not know recent information unless connected to updated sources.

Not always correct

It can produce confident-sounding mistakes or hallucinations.
Open: Why confident language can be risky

LLMs are trained to generate language that fits the prompt and surrounding context. That means the response may sound polished even when the facts are incomplete or wrong.

This is why verification matters, especially for schoolwork, health, law, finance, safety, or anything that affects real decisions.
