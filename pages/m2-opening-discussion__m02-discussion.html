MODULE 2 · OPENING DISCUSSION

Two students. Different examples. Same model?

Before we audit data, defend a choice.

5 Points · Graded
Examples. Patterns. Choices.

Two students teach an AI to spot fresh fruit. The fruit looks the same. The data does not.

The Scenario

Two students train an AI to tell ripe apples from unripe apples. Student A captures 30 apples from a single tree in their backyard. Student B captures 30 apples from 5 different grocery stores in different neighborhoods.

Whose AI will actually work in the real world, and what is each student giving up?

Think about:

• Volume vs variety: Both have 30 samples. The samples are very different. Why does that matter?
• Hidden assumptions: What is each student assuming about how their model will be used?
• Failure modes: Where will each model fail first, and who pays when it does?

Your Task

  1. Initial post (3 to 5 sentences): Pick the student you think trained a better model. Defend the choice. Name what was given up.
  2. Reply to TWO classmates (2 to 3 sentences each): Find a hole in their argument. Be specific about where the data will fail them.
  3. Be specific. Name a real situation where one model would work and the other would not.
