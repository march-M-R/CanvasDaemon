Before You Watch

Stakes set the bar. Not vibes.

“A 90% model is fine for sorting your own photos. A 99% model is unacceptable for diagnosing disease. The accuracy required depends on what happens when the model is wrong.”

Concept Companion

Three tiers of stakes.

Pick the tier your model is actually in, set the accuracy bar accordingly, and add the level of human oversight the tier demands.

Personal use, demo

Low stakes

80 to 90% is fine. No human review required. Worst case is mild inconvenience.

Customer-facing feature

Medium stakes

Higher accuracy + 'I don't know' fallback. Sampled human review.

Safety, health, legal

High stakes

Very high accuracy plus mandatory human review of every decision.

Compare

Same accuracy, different verdicts.

An 85% model can be celebrated in one context and dangerous in another. The number is not the answer.
