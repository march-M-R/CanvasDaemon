Before You Watch

The model only sees pixels.

Your eye adjusts for light, angle, and context without thinking. A vision model sees only pixels. Pixels change wildly across conditions. The model treats them as a new object.

Concept Companion

Three conditions that change everything.

These are the three knobs that move pixels the most. If your training data does not cover them, your test results will not survive contact with the real world.

Different colors and shadows

Lighting

A red mug in warm light and cool light is two different objects in pixels.

Different shapes appear

Angle

A top-down sneaker is unrecognizable next to a side-view sneaker.

Part of the subject is hidden

Occlusion

A hand, a reflection, a crop. Partial views fail unless they were trained on.

Compare

Trained vs untrained conditions.

A model trained at one desk works at that desk. Move it and the failures begin to map out cleanly.

Test conditionAccuracyLesson
Same desk, same light as training92%Don't celebrate. This is essentially training data.
Same desk, dim evening light65%Lighting was not varied in training.
Different room, different background48%Background was the strongest signal in training.
