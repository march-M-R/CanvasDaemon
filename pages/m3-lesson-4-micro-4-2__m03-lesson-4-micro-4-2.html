How Lighting and Angle Change Image Data

Concept Companion

Pixels change. The model sees pixels.

Bright sun, dim lamp, side angle, top-down view, blurry phone shake. Every condition produces different pixel patterns, and a model only knows the patterns it has seen.

Different colors and shadows

Lighting shift

A red mug under warm light and cool light can look like two different mugs.

Different shapes appear

Angle shift

A top-down photo of a sneaker barely resembles a side view.

Part of the subject is hidden

Occlusion

Your hand, glare, or a cropped frame can confuse the model.
