Why Anonymization Is Not Always Anonymous

Two harmless datasets can combine into one identifying dataset.

Big Question

Before You Watch

Re-identification is easier than it sounds.

Research has shown that knowing just three traits (zip code, birth date, gender) can uniquely identify around 80% of people. 'Anonymous' datasets are routinely matched back to real people using public records.

Concept Companion

Three categories of identifier.

Most 'anonymization' removes only the first category. The other two are what re-identifies you.

Names you

Direct ID

Name, email, phone, SSN. Removed by basic anonymization.

Almost names you

Quasi-ID

Zip code, birth date, gender. Looks safe. Identifies most people in combination.

Unique fingerprint

Behavioral

Location traces, browsing patterns, search history. Often unique to one person.
