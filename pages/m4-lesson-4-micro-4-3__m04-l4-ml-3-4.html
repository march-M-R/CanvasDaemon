Big Question

How do you run a risk review that is not just theater?
Watch → Build → Apply

Before You Watch

Specific answers or it does not count.

A useful risk review answers three concrete questions in writing: what could go wrong, who would be hurt, and what we are doing about it. Vague answers fail. Specific answers are the deliverable.

Micro-Learning Video 4.3

Concept Companion

Three questions. Specific each time.

The three-question framework is short on purpose. Long risk reviews hide things. Short, specific reviews force the team to commit.

What can go wrong

Harms

Specific failure modes. Not 'the model could be biased.'

Who is hurt

Affected groups

Name people. Applicants, patients, students, employees, communities.

What we will do

Mitigations

Concrete actions: human review, opt-outs, audits, fallbacks, kill switches.

Compare

Theater review vs real review.

Both look like risk reviews. Only one actually reduces risk.
