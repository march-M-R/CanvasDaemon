Running an AI Risk Review

Concept Companion

Name harms, name groups, name mitigations.

A useful risk review answers three concrete questions in writing: what could go wrong, who would be hurt, and what we are doing about it. Vague answers fail. Specific answers are the deliverable.

What can go wrong

Harms

List specific failure modes: false denials, leaks, stereotyped outputs, missed detections.

Who is hurt

Affected groups

Name the people, not 'users'. Be specific: applicants, patients, students, employees.

What we will do

Mitigations

Concrete actions: human review, opt-outs, audits, fallbacks, kill switches.
