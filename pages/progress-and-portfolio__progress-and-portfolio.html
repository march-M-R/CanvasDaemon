Portfolio Growth

Your work tells the story of your progress.

Instead of seeing each assignment as separate, think of each artifact as one piece of a growing portfolio. By the end, the portfolio should show how your thinking, technical skills, and creativity developed across the course.

Step 1

Understand the ideas.

You begin by learning how AI systems work, where they are used, and how to evaluate them carefully.

Step 2

Practice with tools and code.

You then move from explanation to application through labs, AI workflows, and guided technical practice.

Step 3

Build something real.

You finish by designing and presenting a complete AI web application that reflects your own ideas and decisions.

What We Expect

Three expectations. Nothing more.

The goal is not perfection from day one. The goal is thoughtful progress, responsible AI use, and steady improvement.

1

Stay curious.

Ask questions, test assumptions, and look for the reasoning behind each answer.

2

Use AI critically.

Use AI tools, but verify their outputs, identify their limits, and understand what they produce.

3

Save your work.

Each artifact becomes part of your portfolio and shows how your thinking develops over time.

Portfolio Mindset

Every module adds one more proof of growth.

By the end of the course, your portfolio should show not only what you built, but how you learned to think with AI, question AI, and apply AI responsibly.
